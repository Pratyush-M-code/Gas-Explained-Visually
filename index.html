Gas Explained Visually

Welcome to Gas Explained Visually!

More often than not, many of us learn these laws by rote memorization, without ever seeing how the substances and gases actually interact in reality. We never develop an intuitive feel for these processes.
So to help with that, I've built this website to give people a more concise and intuitive understanding of the laws that govern our world. This series focuses on the Gaseous State and Thermodynamics.

How to use this website?

This is inspired by Many Tiny Things and is mostly meant to be read in a page-by-page format, with navigation at the bottom. However, each page is also a standalone simulation and introduces new topics and ideas. As always, this is an intentionally simplified approach to simulations. Many of the units used, like Kelvin, Joules etc., may not exactly correlate to their real-world counterparts, but the ideas and intuition remain the same!
